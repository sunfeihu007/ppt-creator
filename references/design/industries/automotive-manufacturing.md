# 行业视觉修饰：汽车与制造

## 提示词片段

```
INDUSTRY VISUAL — AUTOMOTIVE AND MANUFACTURING. Express engineering precision,
modularity and reliability through assembly-grid geometry, orthogonal system lines,
machine/part contours, technical section marks and steel-like neutral surfaces. Use real
factory, production-line, equipment or product imagery when supplied. Keep technical
labels clean and functional; do not turn them into a sci-fi HUD. Avoid decorative robots,
fictional gauges and unrelated futuristic factories.
```
