# 行业视觉修饰：金融

## 提示词片段

```
INDUSTRY VISUAL — FINANCE. Express trust, precision, governance and restraint through
institutional grids, hairline rules, compact labels, disciplined tables/charts and clean
document-like surfaces. Prefer verified product interfaces, evidence diagrams and precise
data graphics over decorative illustration. Use minimal radius and almost no shadow.
Avoid sci-fi HUDs, glowing model brains, oversized metallic gold, generic bankers and
luxury symbolism.
```
