# 行业视觉修饰：通用商务

## 提示词片段

```
INDUSTRY VISUAL — GENERAL BUSINESS. Keep the visual language neutral, professional and
content-led. Use abstract structure, verified product imagery, simple geometric diagrams
and restrained business photography only when they add information. Do not introduce an
industry-specific object, fake logo, decorative AI brain, robot, handshake or city skyline
unless the supplied content explicitly requires it.
```
